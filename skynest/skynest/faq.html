Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about stays, pricing and booking.

What is the minimum stay?

Short-Term Stay starts from one week. Premium and Standard stays are monthly.

Are utilities included?

Yes. Water, electricity and Wi-Fi are included in the Premium and Standard packages.

Is the room furnished?

Every room is fully furnished. Linens are included with Short-Term Stays.

How do I book?

Send an enquiry through the Contact page. We reply within one working day to confirm availability and your move-in date.

Is there a deposit?

A refundable security deposit applies to monthly stays. We confirm the amount when you enquire.

Can I get an accessible room?

Yes. Step-free accessible rooms are available through the Custom Package. Tell us what you need and we will arrange it.
